How it works
Search

Iterative deepening alpha–beta with a transposition table, null-move and late-move reductions, and a quiescence search for captures. Moves are ordered by hash move, captures and killer history.

Evaluation

Material plus piece-square tables, blended between a middlegame and an endgame set by the phase of the game. Small terms for pawn structure, king safety and mobility. Weights tuned on 1.2 million positions.

Time management

Each move gets a soft budget from the clock and the increment, and a hard budget twice that. The search finishes its iteration at the soft limit unless the best move is unstable; it stops at the hard limit regardless.

Safety wrapper

A watchdog around the engine process: it validates every move, falls back to the last completed iteration on a timeout, and always answers before the clock runs out.
