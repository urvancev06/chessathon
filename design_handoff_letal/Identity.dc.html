A chess engine's identity, built for a favicon and an app header. One mark, one wordmark, three colours. The mark is the knight's move drawn on a fragment of the board; the wordmark is the system sans, with one drawn letter.

03 · Wordmark
Mikhail Leal

System sans, medium weight, tracking −1.5 %. No external font: the wordmark is live text and takes the platform's sans.

The T is the one drawn letter. Its stem and crossbar are two rectangles in a 0.6 em box; the crossbar runs on over “al” as a rank line, so the champion's name sits under it.

Always set as one line. Never letterspaced wider, never bold, never in the accent colour.
